# Bitcoin Price Volatility: A Comparative GARCH Family Analysis

This repository contains the research paper:

*"Capturing Asymmetry and Heavy Tails in Bitcoin Price Volatility – A Comparative GARCH Family Analysis"*  
by Suha Roy and Abhishek Gupta  
M.Sc. Computer Science (Big Data Analytics)  
Central University of Rajasthan  

---

## Abstract

This study analyzes Bitcoin’s weekly returns from March 2020 to March 2025 using a suite of GARCH-family models, including:

- GARCH(1,1) with Normal and Student’s t-distributions
- EGARCH(1,1) with Normal and Student’s t-distributions

The empirical results indicate that *EGARCH(1,1) with Student’s t-distribution* provides the best overall fit. The findings emphasize the critical role of modeling *asymmetry* and *heavy tails* in crypto-market volatility forecasting.

---

## Highlights

- *Data*: 5-year weekly closing prices of Bitcoin
- *Models Compared*: GARCH vs EGARCH; Normal vs Student's t errors
- *Model Selection Criteria*: AIC, BIC, Log-Likelihood
- *Forecast*: 8-week out-of-sample volatility forecasts
- *Findings*: Student's t-distribution + EGARCH captures extreme volatility better
